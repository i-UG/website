About i-UG

A community built for IBM i in the UK

i-UG is the United Kingdom's independent user group for the IBM i platform. We bring together developers, systems architects, CIOs and the businesses that rely on IBM i to share knowledge, shape best practice and strengthen the platform's future.

Through conferences, regional meet-ups, technical workshops and an active online network, we help members stay current with modernisation, security, RPG, Db2 for i and the wider IBM Power ecosystem.

Our mission

To connect, educate and advocate for the UK IBM i community, ensuring organisations get the most from their investment in the platform and that skills continue to thrive across generations of professionals.
